Privacy & Data Security

Privacy Policy

DailyFlow is built with an offline-first, zero-tracking philosophy.

🔒
100% Local Device Storage

All your tasks, daily quick notes, Pomodoro records, and finance transactions are saved exclusively on your local device storage. Your personal information never leaves your phone or browser.

🛡️
Zero Tracking & No Remote Servers

DailyFlow does not operate remote user tracking databases or transmit telemetry. We never collect, monitor, share, or sell any personal or financial data to third parties.

🔔
On-Device Local Notifications

Task reminders and alarms are scheduled directly through your device's operating system (Local Notifications). No external cloud servers receive or process your reminder schedule.

🗑️
Complete User Ownership

You have full sovereignty over your data at all times. Clearing application data or uninstalling the app permanently purges all stored entries from your device.

Questions or inquiries about our privacy practices? Contact us at ctrlshiftplay01@gmail.com.
